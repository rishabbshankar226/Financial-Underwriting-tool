# Policy and regulatory source register

Checked 2026-09-08. These links document what the prototype referenced; they are not legal advice.

- SBA SOP 50 10 versions: https://legacy.sba.gov/document/sop-50-10-lender-development-company-loan-programs — Version 8 effective 2025-06-01; Version 8.1 published effective 2026-10-01.
- SBA size standards open data: https://data.sba.gov/dataset/small-business-size-standards — current workbook identified as effective 2023-03-17.
- Regulation B §1002.9: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/9/ — adverse-action reasons must relate to factors actually considered/scored.
- Regulation Z §1026.43: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/43/ — ability-to-repay / qualified mortgage framework; General QM is not a fixed 43% DTI rule.
- Federal Reserve SR 26-2: https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm — issued 2026-04-17 and supersedes SR 11-7.
- FTC Safeguards Rule: https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/safeguards-rule — 16 CFR Part 314.
